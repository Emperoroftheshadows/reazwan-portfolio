Portfolio Projects & Designs

Steering Knuckle Design

Redesigned a JLR rear suspension knuckle from cast steel to A356 aluminium — ~66% weight reduction. Validated with static and frequency-response FEA in ANSYS. Max stress at 13% of yield, FoS above 5. Led DFMEA across a five-person team.

Dissertation: Tennis Ball Launcher

Designed, manufactured and tested a dual-wheel BLDC launcher exploiting the Magnus effect within a £300 budget. Modelled in SolidWorks, FDM-printed in PETG. Custom 4:1 planetary gearbox delivering 1.62 Nm torque, validated at 30–40 mph.

Submarine CFD Analysis

Steady-state RANS CFD of a full-scale submarine at 20 km/h using Ansys Fluent. 6.27M-element mesh; drag converged to ~33,500 N. Stagnation pressure matched theory within 0.03%. Conning tower identified as primary drag source.

IMechE Design Challenge

Led a six-engineer team to design an autonomous wall-impact return vehicle for IMechE 2023–24. Rack-and-pinion distance measurement with motor polarity reversal — zero post-activation intervention, within a £40 budget.

Portable Gantry Crane

Designed and FEA-validated a portable gantry crane to BS EN 1991/1993. Bending stress 1.40 MPa (FoS = 253), deflection 0.014 mm (FoS = 167). ANSYS FEA validated within 1%. Modal analysis confirmed no resonance risk. Drawings to BS 8888.

Wind Tunnel

Designed and built a wind tunnel using 3D-printed components, 12V DC fans, and ultrasonic misters. Arduino and ESP32 for real-time control; results validated with ANSYS/SolidWorks CFD simulations to analyse aerodynamic performance.
