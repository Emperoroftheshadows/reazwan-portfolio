Portfolio Projects & Designs

Jaguar Land Rover Steering Knuckle

Redesigned a JLR rear suspension knuckle from cast steel to A356 aluminium — approximately 66% weight reduction. Validated with static and frequency-response FEA in ANSYS, with maximum stress at 13% of yield and a factor of safety above 5.

Dissertation: Tennis Ball Launcher

Designed, manufactured and tested a dual-wheel BLDC launcher exploiting the Magnus effect within a £300 budget. Modelled in SolidWorks, FDM-printed in PETG, and validated at 30–40 mph.

Submarine CFD Analysis

Steady-state RANS CFD of a full-scale submarine at 20 km/h using Ansys Fluent. A 6.27-million-element mesh produced a converged drag result of approximately 33,500 N.

IMechE Design Challenge

Portable Gantry Crane

Wind Tunnel
