Submarine CFD Analysis

Steady-state RANS simulation of a full-scale submarine fully submerged at 20 km/h (5.556 m/s) using Ansys Fluent 2024 R2 with the k-ω SST turbulence model. A Reynolds number of 1.216 × 10⁸ confirmed fully turbulent flow across the hull. A 6.27-million-element unstructured tetrahedral mesh with an average orthogonal quality of 0.767 was generated to resolve the boundary layer and wake regions accurately.

Drag converged to approximately 33,500 N and lift to approximately 3,500 N downward. The key validation came from the bow stagnation pressure: 15,410 Pa simulated against 15,414 Pa theoretical — a 0.03% error that confirmed the solver setup and mesh were correctly capturing the fundamental flow physics.

Technical breakdown

Mesh & Setup

A multi-zone unstructured tetrahedral mesh was generated in Ansys Meshing — fine element density at the hull surface, conning tower, and antenna appendages, coarsening progressively through the far field. The 6.27M-element mesh achieved an average orthogonal quality of 0.767. Boundary conditions: velocity inlet at 5.556 m/s, pressure outlet, symmetry plane along the hull centreline, and no-slip walls on all submarine surfaces.

Flow Results

Velocity streamlines showed fully attached flow along the main cylindrical hull and clean acceleration around the sail geometry. Drag converged to ~33,500 N and lift to ~3,500 N downward. The stagnation pressure at the bow nose — 15,410 Pa simulated vs 15,414 Pa theoretical — validated the simulation to 0.03% of the analytical prediction, confirming correct solver configuration and mesh resolution.

Pressure & Drag Analysis

The 3D pressure contour mapped onto the submarine surface identifies the primary drag sources: the red high-pressure stagnation region at the bow, and the marked low-pressure zones behind the conning tower and aft appendages. Streamline analysis confirmed these appendages generate disproportionate drag through flow separation relative to their size — the dominant design consideration for submarine appendage geometry optimisation.
